Bravick

An offline-first school management and computer-based testing platform for West African schools, built for settings where power and internet are unreliable.

React + Vite, Node/Express, SQLite, Electron

Faith in Practice: Bravick serves schools without steady power or internet, a way of reaching the one.

Evidence-Following in RAG

A published benchmark that measures whether large language models follow the evidence they are given, rather than adding confident claims the evidence does not support.

Zenodo, doi:10.5281/zenodo.22151237 · View the record

Faith in Practice: This work asks whether AI stays honest with the evidence it is given, so a student or farmer can trust the answer.
