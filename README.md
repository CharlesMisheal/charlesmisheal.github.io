## Research Interests
I study whether AI systems are reliable and trustworthy, especially whether language models follow the evidence they are given. I'm particularly interested in calibrated evidence-following in retrieval-augmented and agentic systems, robustness to prompt injection and poisoned retrieval, and AI in education for learners in Africa.
