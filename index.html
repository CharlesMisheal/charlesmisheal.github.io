Charles Ahuose Misheal

I build technology that keeps working when the internet doesn't, and I test whether AI tells the truth.

I once planned to study medicine. When that door closed, I taught myself technology through YouTube, and I have been teaching others ever since. Today I am a final-year B.A.S. Information Technology student at Ensign College (GPA 3.66, expected August 2027), a Certified Kubernetes Administrator, the founder of CloudCity Technology and Education, and a former IT Manager at Golden Touch College.

I built Bravick, an offline-first school management and exam platform for schools with unreliable internet. I also published a benchmark showing that AI models which passed up to 103 of 105 evidence tests with a careful prompt passed as few as 9 with a plain one. That gap is why I am heading into AI evaluation and research, and into AI for education that serves African learners.

  • 300+ learners mentored over 5+ years of teaching
  • Published LLM evaluation benchmark on Zenodo
  • 0.49 → 0.74 nDCG@5 as team lead on a retrieval pipeline
