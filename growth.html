Mentorship and Growth

These SMART goals keep my professional certification work and my spiritual portfolio moving together through IT 497 Block 6.

SMART goal 1: Azure Administrator (AZ-104)

Specific
Pass the Microsoft Azure Administrator (AZ-104) certification exam.
Measurable
Success is a passing exam result from Microsoft.
Achievable
I am already preparing for AZ-104 as part of my cloud studies and daily work with Azure.
Relevant
The credential strengthens the cloud skills I use in my work and will need after graduation.
Time-bound
Pass the exam by the end of IT 497 Block 6.

SMART goal 2: Ongoing spiritual portfolio practice

Specific
Keep an ongoing spiritual goal of writing regular Faith and Technology reflections in this portfolio.
Measurable
Publish bi-weekly portfolio entries, with at least three completed by the end of Block 6.
Achievable
A short, scheduled entry every two weeks fits beside coursework, work, and scripture study.
Relevant
Consistent writing puts the principle of small and simple things into practice and keeps faith connected to my professional growth.
Time-bound
At least three entries by the end of IT 497 Block 6, continuing on a bi-weekly rhythm.
