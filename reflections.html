Faith and Technology Reflections

This page collects short reflections on how faith and technology meet in my studies and work. I add a new post every two weeks.

How this portfolio fits together

My Spiritual Progression Portfolio builds in layers. Each part grows out of the one before it:

  1. Guiding Principles: the three principles that guide my growth, with the materials that support them.
  2. Faith and Competencies: how five of my professional competencies align with those principles.
  3. Projects: where the principles show up in real work, with ethics and faith in practice.
  4. Integration Reflection: a mind map of how I keep Christ at the center, with warning signs and realignment strategies.
  5. Mentorship and Growth: SMART goals that keep my professional and spiritual growth moving together.
  6. Spiritual Portfolio Review: findings and enhancements that guide the finalization of this portfolio.
